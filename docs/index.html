Welcome to Veegil Election Project

Empowering Citizens Through Data-Driven Democracy

Veegil Election is your trusted platform for exploring election and voting data in Nigeria. We bring transparency, clarity, and insight to the democratic process by making electoral information accessible, interactive, and easy to understand.

Whether you’re a voter, researcher, journalist, or policymaker, our tools help you:

  • Navigate and explore detailed polling unit maps by state
  • Visualize voter turnout, what-if-analyses and election results through infographics
  • Learn more about the electoral process, and how your vote counts

Visit the GitHub repository for details.
